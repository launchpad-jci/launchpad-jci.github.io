Welcome

What is Launchpad?

Launchpad sets young adults up for success now, instead of leaving them to work it out the hard way over the next 10 to 15 years.

It is a personal, one-on-one program. The main focus is money: budgeting, tax, super, investing and the habits that build wealth quietly in the background. Around that we cover the parts of adult life school doesn't teach.

We also discuss simple set and forget systems to put in place, so good habits keep working without willpower. You leave with clear goals and the confidence to own your finances.

  • Money basics
  • Tax
  • Super and investing
  • Insurance and buying a home
  • Small business
  • Grants for business ideas
  • Your rights
  • Life admin and wellbeing

You choose the topics, and your session is built around them.

Four steps

How it works

  1. 1

    The questionnaire

    A PDF you fill in on your phone: what you have in place, the topics you want, your goals and your questions.

  2. 2

    Your money snapshot and budget

    An assessment of your current financial position, turned into a simple working budget.

  3. 3

    Your one-on-one session

    1.5 hours on Zoom, from anywhere in Australia. We set short and long term financial goals, answer your questions and set things up so you leave with ownership of your plan.

  4. 4

    Your summary, guide and goals

    It doesn't finish at 1.5 hours. Afterwards you receive a written summary of what we discussed, a guide to follow and your goals.

Who runs it

About Mitch, and why Launchpad was created

I'm Mitch. I come from a diverse working background, both blue collar and white collar: mining, tourism, business, community engagement, technology, defence, health and education, to name a few. I have worked for an employer or run my own business since I was 13, and since 17 I have never had fewer than two jobs at once.

I'm a university graduate, a coach, and still work as a paramedic. I run business initiative and activation projects for councils and businesses, I'm involved in venture capital, commercial property and business management, and I own and run three businesses, including Launchpad.

I was lucky to be taught about money early, and to have fantastic mentors who guided me wisely. As a parent of two, I understand the weight parents carry in making sure their kids are ready for life. School provides an education, but most don't provide guidance for the real life decisions that come after it. Taught early, those decisions can build a stable platform for a successful and financially secure life. That is where Launchpad was born.

Empowerment and confidence, not financial advice

Launchpad is about financial empowerment and confidence, not financial advice. Everything on this page, in the questionnaire and in the sessions is general information only. It is not personal financial, tax or legal advice, and it is not professional life advice or counselling. We explain how things work so you can make your own informed decisions, and those decisions are yours. Investing carries risk and no result is guaranteed. Who to see for professional advice, and when, is discussed in your session.
